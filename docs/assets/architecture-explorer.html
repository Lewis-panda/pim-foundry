What was actually run

Each placement evaluates the real tensor graph once on the CPU, checks an independent scalar oracle, verifies a sealed bundle, and replays it. One operation profile predicts three identical requests arriving at t = 0 with at most three admitted at a time.

All devices use the same illustrative coefficients: 16 MAC/ns, 16 elements/ns, 64 local bytes/ns, 10 ns launch cost and 4 KiB capacity. Each directed link uses 16 bytes/ns plus 5 ns latency. Energy coefficients are zero assumptions and no energy result is claimed.

The timing is an uncalibrated analytical prediction. This example executes no native simulator or physical accelerator and does not evaluate task quality. Native SRAM and HBM adapters are separate supported interfaces.
